How confirmation works

  1. When a record is due, a full register would remind the owner. This demonstrator shows the states but sends no reminders.
  2. The owner confirms the record is still accurate, or corrects it. Owner-hosted records are confirmed by updating the date in their own solution.yml.
  3. After {{ config.stale_after_months }} months without confirmation the record is marked stale. It stays visible.

In this demonstrator the thresholds are {{ config.due_after_months }} and {{ config.stale_after_months }} months. The working group may choose different ones.{% if config.examples_as_of %} Fictional examples are shown as they would be on {{ config.examples_as_of | long_date }}, so that each keeps showing the state it was written to show.{% endif %}
