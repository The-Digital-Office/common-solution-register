Solutions, codebases and publiccode.yml

A common solution is what an owner offers for reuse. Its code, if it has any, is one or more codebases. The register describes solutions with solution.yml, and links to their codebases, which keep using the European publiccode.yml standard unchanged.

Questionsolution.ymlpubliccode.yml
DescribesA common solution: what is offered, by whom, and how to adopt itA codebase: one software repository
LivesWherever the owner chooses, or in the registerAt the root of the codebase's repository
How manyOne per solutionOne per codebase. A solution may have none, one or several.
RelationshipLists its codebasesValidated by the register when a solution links to it
