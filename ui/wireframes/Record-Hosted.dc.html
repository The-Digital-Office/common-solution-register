About this solution

A demonstrator of a register of digital solutions intended for reuse across Scottish public bodies.

[Purpose from solution.yml: helps public bodies discover intentionally reusable digital assets and make an initial assessment of whether, and how, to adopt them. Maintained as structured records from which a website, open API and bulk data extract are generated.]
