How confirmation works

  1. When a record is due, a full register would remind the owner. This demonstrator shows the states but sends no reminders.
  2. The owner confirms the record is still accurate, or corrects it. Owner-hosted records are confirmed by updating the date in their own solution.yml.
  3. After 12 months without confirmation the record is marked stale. It stays visible.

In this demonstrator the thresholds are 11 and 12 months. The working group may choose different ones.
