What is a common solution?

A service, platform, component, API, standard or pattern that one public body offers for others to reuse, instead of each building its own.

How the register works

Who is this register for?

If you plan, buy or build digital services in the Scottish public sector, it helps you find what already exists, who owns it and how to adopt it. If you own a solution, it is where you describe it.
